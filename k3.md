# Kaizen 3

## Model-specific pre-kitted small-parts tray

**Current issue**  
Small parts are picked separately from a low tray area or requested from warehouse support during the build. This causes extra bending, turning, delays, and interruptions to warehouse personnel.

**Proposed improvement**  
Create a model-specific pre-kitted small-parts tray that travels with the pump or docks at the station.

**Why this would help**  
- Reduces bending and turning
- Lowers part-pick mistakes
- Reduces missing parts during assembly
- Cuts delays from part requests
- Reduces interruptions to warehouse support

**Main benefit**  
Better material control, smoother flow, and less disruption to both assembly and warehouse operations.
