# Kaizen 4

## Swivel small-parts tray add-on for oven cart

**Current issue**  
The oven cart moves the main pump parts well, but the small parts are still picked from a low area off to the side. This causes bending, turning, and breaks the flow of assembly.

**Proposed improvement**  
Add a swivel-mounted small-parts tray to the modular oven cart with a flip-down support arm or locking support.

**Why this would help**  
- Keeps small parts with the pump being built
- Reduces bending and turning
- Keeps parts closer to the assembler
- Improves flow during assembly
- Uses the current cart design without a full redesign

**Main benefit**  
A simple cart add-on that makes the current setup more efficient and easier to work from.
