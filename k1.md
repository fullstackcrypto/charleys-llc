# Kaizen 1

## Adjustable operator platform

**Current issue**  
Some refrigeration assembly stations sit too low for taller assemblers, which causes repeated bending, poor posture, and extra fatigue through the shift.

**Proposed improvement**  
Add an adjustable operator platform at selected stations so the working height can better match the assembler.

**Why this would help**  
- Reduces bending and strain
- Improves posture for taller operators
- Makes assembly work more consistent
- Helps efficiency over a full shift

**Main benefit**  
A simple ergonomic improvement that can make the work smoother, safer, and less fatiguing without changing the full station layout.
