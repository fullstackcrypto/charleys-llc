# Kaizen 2

## Elevated small-parts tray

**Current issue**  
Small parts are picked from a low side area, which causes repeated turning, bending, and lost motion during assembly.

**Proposed improvement**  
Add an elevated small-parts tray at the station so the parts stay closer to the assembler and within the main work zone.

**Why this would help**  
- Reduces bending and turning
- Keeps parts closer to the build area
- Improves part access and flow
- Cuts wasted motion during assembly

**Main benefit**  
A simple way to improve ergonomics and keep the assembly process smoother and more efficient.
