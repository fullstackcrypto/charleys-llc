# Kaizen 6

## Station-specific tool and consumable layout

**Current issue**  
Common tools, grease, fasteners, and small-use items are not always placed within the best reach zone for the assembler, especially for taller operators.

**Proposed improvement**  
Standardize a station-specific layout with raised tool holders, labeled positions, and better placement for common consumables.

**Why this would help**  
- Reduces reaching and searching
- Improves 5S and organization
- Keeps tools easier to return and find
- Improves repeatability
- Reduces wasted motion through the shift

**Main benefit**  
A cleaner and more efficient station layout that supports better flow, better ergonomics, and less wasted movement.
