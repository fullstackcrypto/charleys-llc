# Kaizen 5

## Visual parts tray and step reference

**Current issue**  
Assemblers may still rely on memory or separate instructions for part ID, quantity, and build sequence, especially during training or when switching variants.

**Proposed improvement**  
Add a visual reference card or tray insert showing part image, part name or number, quantity, and step order.

**Why this would help**  
- Improves training
- Reduces part confusion
- Helps sequence accuracy
- Improves consistency between assemblers
- Reduces mistakes on similar-looking parts

**Main benefit**  
A simple visual aid that supports training, accuracy, and more repeatable assembly.
